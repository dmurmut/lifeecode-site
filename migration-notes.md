## Original Public-Site Visual Reference

The original LifeeCode site uses a **light clinical pastel system**, rather than parchment and eucalyptus. Its key visual identifiers are a pale blue-to-mint page backdrop, white rounded content cards with thin lavender or pink borders, compact centered navigation, and bright gradient or solid action buttons in cyan, rose, violet, and green. The hero is a single wide, very light wearable-tech image with centered, dark serif headline copy placed over it.

The original homepage uses a narrow all-white header, a bright orange-to-magenta Hindi announcement bar, a centered hero with four action buttons, and compact icon-led cards. Content pages keep a spacious centered content column with a small colored category pill, large classic serif heading, rounded white episode/brief cards, and accent-color badges. The preferred restoration target is **visual fidelity to these original conventions**, while retaining the recovered site’s migrated content and routes.
