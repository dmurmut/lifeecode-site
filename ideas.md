# LifeeCode Website Recovery — Design Direction

## Three Candidate Approaches

### 1. Clinical Editorial Garden
**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, research-led publication expressed through a bright botanical lens. It makes high-stakes health technology feel legible, personal, and human.
**Probability:** 0.06

### 2. Signal Desk
**Very Brief Intro:** A restrained intelligence-brief aesthetic with clear data markers and an analyst's point of view. It frames each weekly update as a decision-relevant signal rather than a headline.
**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Warm Precision
**Very Brief Intro:** A magazine-like wellness experience that combines compassionate writing with precise evidence cues. It uses natural materials and measured structure rather than clinical sterility.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Direction: Clinical Editorial Garden

### Design Movement
**Contemporary editorial wellness**, combining the compositional confidence of a premium magazine with the clarity and trust signals of evidence-based healthcare communication.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence is visible:** Sources, issue numbers, and concise labels make each claim feel accountable without overwhelming the reader.
2. **Wellness is human:** Soft natural imagery, generous breathing room, and considered language keep the experience warm instead of medicalized.
3. **Signals over noise:** Hierarchy focuses attention on the current brief and the single most relevant action.
4. **Purposeful asymmetry:** Offset editorial blocks and slim annotation rails create movement and avoid a generic centered landing page.

### Color Philosophy
The page begins in **warm parchment** to signal reflection and readable editorial content. A restrained **eucalyptus green** anchors trust and action, while **marigold** acts as an optimistic signal color for newly published material. Charcoal ink gives long-form copy the visual authority of print. The colors should feel restorative, not technological for technology's sake.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an **editorial field-note composition**: a narrow vertical signal rail intersects a wide content canvas, while the hero uses left-aligned copy across a low-key health-tech image. Content moves between a featured story band, staggered topic cards, and a bottom call-to-listen panel; it should not resolve into a repetitive central grid.

### Signature Elements
1. A slim **Signal No. / Week** annotation rail with a small round status marker.
2. **Botanical data lines**—fine stem-like rules and tiny nodes used as dividers and section anchors.
3. Large, italicized **editorial pull phrases** beside practical evidence blocks.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions remain quiet and useful. Links underline with a short eucalyptus sweep; cards lift only slightly while their signal marker brightens. Navigation stays direct and never competes with the week’s main finding.

### Animation
Use a 450ms editorial reveal for hero copy, with a 55ms stagger for individual labels and actions. Divider nodes may travel a few pixels along their line on hover. Maintain motion below 300ms for controls, animate only transform and opacity, and disable non-essential animation for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for major headlines and pull phrases; its high-contrast shapes create editorial gravity. Pair it with **Manrope** for labels, summaries, navigation, and body copy. Headlines use a tight, expressive scale; body text remains at a generous 16–18px with ample line-height. Use uppercase Manrope labels sparingly with expanded tracking for issue metadata.

### Brand Essence
**LifeeCode turns fast-moving health technology into calm, privacy-first evidence you can use.**

Personality: **considered, protective, lucid.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive and specific; CTAs are invitations to understand rather than commands to convert. Microcopy names the reader’s real concern—health, time, privacy, and uncertainty—without hype.

Examples:

> **Five health-tech signals worth your attention this week.**

> **See what changed—and what it means for your data, body, and choices.**

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark treats **LifeeCode** as a refined editorial masthead, pairing a softly seriffed “Lifee” with a measured sans “Code.” The independent mark is a bold, abstract **leaf-and-circuit glyph**: three tapering green arcs enclosing a warm central signal dot, suggesting protected health intelligence without using a cliché heartbeat icon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Eucalyptus — #2D6A5A.** A grounded, ownable green used for key actions, data markers, and trust cues.

## Style Decisions

- Preserve the proven public information architecture: Home, Weekly Briefs, Podcast, Hindi, and About.
- Promote the latest published episode and four primary distribution destinations: Spotify, YouTube, Apple Podcasts, and weekly briefs.
- Do not imitate the old implementation pixel-for-pixel; retain its calm editorial intent while establishing a more deliberate, accessible recovery build.
