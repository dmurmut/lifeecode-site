| Digitally derived measures | [FDA paper](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/digital-health-center-excellence/key-considerations-development-and-use-digitally-derived-measures-clinical-investigations-fda-paper) | Digital health technologies can create continuously collected measures for clinical investigations. | Candidate biomarkers | [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/an-ai-tool-for-prioritizing-candidate-biomarkers-from-wearable-sensor-data/) | The Biomarker Discovery Framework uses human-supervised, multi-agent hypothesis generation and adversarial validation across three cohorts totaling 9,279 participant-observations. Candidates remain research candidates, not clinical diagnoses. |
| Patient trust in health AI | [JMIR systematic review](https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e85663/) | A 25-study, 528-participant review identified privacy, reliability, black-box decision-making, accountability, empathy, bias, and access concerns as systemic rather than superficial issues. |
