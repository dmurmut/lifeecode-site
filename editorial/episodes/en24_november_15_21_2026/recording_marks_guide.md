# Recording Marks Guide — LifeeCode Episode 24

**Coverage:** Sunday 15 November – Saturday 21 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Script:** `episode24_audio_only_script.md` (video: `episode24_faceless_video_master_script.md`)

## Delivery tone

Warm, clear, unhurried — a trusted explainer, not an alarm. The deep dive is good news with a catch: let the first half sound genuinely positive, then slow down and lower intensity for the "outside HIPAA" turn. Signal 3 (emotional-support chatbots) is gentle and caring throughout. Signal 4 (breach) is matter-of-fact, never dramatic.

## Pacing target

135–145 words per minute; about 10 minutes for the audio script. Deep dive ≈ 4 minutes; each signal 50–70 seconds; Privacy Radar ≈ 1 minute.

## Deep-dive priority marks

1. "This rule is **final**, / but it is **not in force yet**." // — the status line must land clearly.
2. "fifteen calendar days / to respond, / down from **thirty**" — bright, positive.
3. "Now, the hidden risk. // Take a breath here" — real pause.
4. <slow>"once you send your records into a consumer app that isn't covered by HIPAA, / HIPAA **no longer protects that copy**."</slow> // — the single most important sentence.
5. "Both things are true." — even, no judgement.
6. "connecting is **optional**." /
7. Futurist line: "protects data based on **who holds it** / — not **what it is**." //

## Pre-record checklist

- [ ] HIPAA rule said as "final, not yet in force"; dates said as "January and July 2027"; never "now requires".
- [ ] No claim that the rule regulates apps or that apps are unsafe in general.
- [ ] Discharge study: no "better than doctors" or "safer for patients".
- [ ] Chatbot study: "association"; no help/harm claim; support-line line delivered gently.
- [ ] Breach: "the company said"; no summed totals; EMR not confirmed.
- [ ] Wearables: "doesn't prove".
- [ ] Medical-information notice read near the start.
- [ ] Momentum: a new fact, consequence or question every 20–30 seconds; no section longer than planned.
