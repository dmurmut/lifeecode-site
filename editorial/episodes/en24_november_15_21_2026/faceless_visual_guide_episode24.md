# Faceless Visual Guide — LifeeCode Episode 24

**Coverage:** Sunday 15 November – Saturday 21 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Deep dive:** Your Records, Your App — and Outside HIPAA: What the New Privacy Rule Changes for You

## Episode visual thesis

**Data crossing a boundary.** One abstract record travels a teal path; when it crosses the HIPAA boundary into a consumer app, its outline turns amber. The visual never implies a breach in the deep dive — the point is a change in protection, not a leak.

## Asset direction

- Build everything from abstract shapes, lines, and type. No stock photos of patients, clinicians, hospitals, or phones with real apps.
- The "building" is a simple rounded outline, not a hospital with a cross or logo.
- The "phone" is a rounded rectangle with no brand features or UI.
- Signal 3 must stay calm: no crying faces, dark rooms, or crisis imagery.
- Signal 4: red only on grid cells; never a hooded hacker, skull, or broken padlock.

## Editing rhythm

- A visual change every 4–6 seconds; a new on-screen fact every 20–30 seconds.
- Hold the "not in force yet" badge for at least 3 seconds.
- Hold the boundary-crossing moment for a beat of silence (0.5–1 s) in the deep dive.
- Signals open with the status badge before any number.

## Accessibility and captions

- Burned-in or closed captions for all narration; speaker-neutral.
- Minimum on-screen text 48 px at 1080p; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (white on navy passes).
- Never encode meaning by colour alone: every rating also shows its word (High / Medium / Low).
- Avoid fast flashing; radar sweep at a slow, steady rate.
- Pronunciations per `recording_marks_guide.md`.
