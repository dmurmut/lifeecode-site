# Recording Marks Guide — LifeeCode Episode 23

**Coverage:** Sunday 8 November – Saturday 14 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Script:** `episode23_audio_only_script.md` (the faceless master script uses the same narration, lightly condensed)

## Delivery tone

Warm, calm, precise. The deep dive is about broken trust, not scandal: sound concerned and fair, never outraged. Signals 2 and 3 are quietly optimistic; Signal 4 is curious; Signal 5 is gentle and low-intensity because it touches mood and mental health.

## Pacing target

135–145 words per minute overall. Slow to ~120 wpm for status lines ("this is a lawsuit…", "conference abstract…") and the action card. Lift to ~150 wpm for lineup and Privacy Radar transitions.

- [ ] **Narration momentum:** a reveal, consequence or question every 20–30 seconds — check the 14 million figure, "Apollo", "not a hack", the 9% disagreement, "never to always".
