**Coverage:** Sunday 8 November – Saturday 14 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Pairs with:** `episode23_faceless_video_master_script.md`

## Episode visual thesis

**A promise and a line that escapes it.** The episode is about the difference between what a health service promises and where data actually travels. One motif carries it: a teal data line that should stay inside a boundary. When it stays in, it is verified and bounded (echo AI, repeat ECG testing). When it leaks toward abstract ad audiences, it turns amber (allegation) — never red unless marking the high privacy-risk rating.

## Asset direction

- Motion graphics and abstract illustration only. Stock footage, if any, limited to non-identifiable hands, phones with blank screens, and soft-focus environments.
- **Never show:** Hims & Hers, Meta, Snap, Apple, Samsung, Oura or Kaiser logos or product shots; real app or chatbot interfaces; real patient records or fake EHR screens; identifiable patients or clinicians; pills or imagery suggesting a specific condition; robot doctors; hacker hoodies, skulls, or "breach" imagery (this is not a breach).
- Checkout mock must be generic, unbranded, and obviously illustrative.
- Watches and ring must be simple outlines, not recognisable products.

## Editing rhythm

- A new visual beat every 4–6 seconds; a reveal (counter, stamp, chip) every 20–30 seconds.
- Hold status chips on screen for at least 3 seconds so they can be read.
- Slow cuts (6–8 s) in Signal 5 and the action card; faster cuts (3–4 s) in the lineup and Privacy Radar.

## Accessibility and captioning

- Burned-in or uploaded captions for all narration; verify spellings: Hims & Hers, Apollo, Icahn, TemPredict, Oura, MeitY, DPDP.
- Minimum on-screen text size equivalent to 42 px at 1080p; contrast at least 4.5:1 (white on navy passes; amber on navy for large text only).
- Never use colour alone to convey risk — always pair the red/amber chip with the word HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW.
- No flashing faster than three times per second.
