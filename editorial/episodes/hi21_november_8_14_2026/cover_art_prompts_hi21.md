## Art selection notes

- **Recommend A:** the hook repeats the title's question, and the phone-plus-health-icons picture says "health data" more clearly than B's generic permission pop-up.
- **Choose B** if you want the cover to feel more like a habit/action episode; its hook is shorter and reads better at thumbnail size.
- **Check after generation:** Devanagari conjuncts and matras (especially "आपकी", "रुकिए"), the em-dash in the title, "EPISODE 21", no stray English mistranslation, no red used anywhere except the hook line (red is justified here as a privacy/risk story).
- Keep lock or shield symbols small and calm; avoid anything that suggests the app or government endorses a product.
