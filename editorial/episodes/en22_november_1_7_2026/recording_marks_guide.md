# Recording Marks Guide — LifeeCode Episode 22

**Coverage:** Sunday 1 November – Saturday 7 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Applies to:** `episode22_audio_only_script.md` (and the narration in the faceless master script)

## Delivery tone

Warm, steady, quietly concerned — never alarmed. The deep dive should sound like a trusted friend explaining a letter that came in the mail, not a crime report. Signals are brisker and curious. The Privacy Radar is calm and practical.

## Pacing target

135–145 words per minute overall (≈10 minutes). Deep dive slightly slower (≈135); signals ≈145; Privacy Radar ≈140 with clear row breaks.

## Deep-dive priority marks

1. "This is a settlement with state attorneys general. / It *isn't* a court finding." — clear, neutral; do not rush.
2. "The company did *not* require multi-factor authentication" — emphasise *not*, then explain the term kindly.
3. "It *saw* them. // But it wasn't set up to alert anyone." — the emotional peak; full beat after "saw them".
4. Data list — slow, one item per pulse; (low) on "Diagnoses. / Lab and diagnostic results. / Medication names."
5. "At least three hundred thousand dollars *now*. / Another nine point seven million / is due *only if* …" — emphasise "now" and "only if" so listeners don't hear "$10 million fine".
6. "One fair caution:" — lower intensity; this is the no-overclaim line.
7. "who else is holding your story?" — slow, reflective; full beat before "Our Privacy Rating: High."

## Pre-record checklist

- [ ] **Evidence language:** "settlement", "the attorneys general say", "a study found", "discussion paper", "certified classes", "plaintiffs allege", "demonstrated" — all read as written.
- [ ] **No overclaim:** no "admitted", "found liable", "$10 million fine", "detects/measures blood pressure", "FDA approved", "Meta broke the law", "rolled out across India".
- [ ] **Sensitivity/specificity:** "caught about sixty-six percent of people who had high blood pressure" and "correctly cleared about ninety percent of people who didn't" — not "accuracy".
- [ ] **Medical caveat:** read in the opening; not skipped or shortened.
- [ ] **Dates:** coverage "first to seventh of November, 2026"; Meta order "thirtieth of October".
- [ ] **Narration momentum:** a new fact, consequence, or question every 20–30 seconds — no flat stretches in the deep dive.
- [ ] **Do not name** any hospital client of the Perry Johnson companies.
