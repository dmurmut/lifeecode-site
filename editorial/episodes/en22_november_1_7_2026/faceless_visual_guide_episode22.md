**Coverage:** Sunday 1 November – Saturday 7 November 2026 (America/New_York)
**Pairs with:** `episode22_faceless_video_master_script.md` · **Runtime:** ~10:30

## Episode visual thesis

**"The weak link is rarely the gadget."** Every segment shows the *hidden layer* around health technology — vendors behind a clinic, a tracker behind a web page, a doctor's sign-off gate in front of a record, a signal line between a surgeon and a robot. Visually: a calm, navy foreground of the thing people see, with a dimmer background layer of the systems they don't. Teal marks bounded, verified flows; amber marks a point needing review; red appears only for the two High-risk items, briefly.

## Asset direction

- Motion graphics and abstract illustration only; no stock footage of patients, clinicians, hospitals, or hackers.
- No real logos: not Perry Johnson, Meta/Facebook, Google, FDA, NHA, or any hospital. Use text names in plain type on status stamps only.
- Devices are unbranded silhouettes. The robot in Signal 3 is an abstract, non-anthropomorphic articulated line form — no faces, no "robot doctor".
- Data chips show category words only ("Diagnosis", "Medication name") — never realistic records, numbers, or names.
- The browser frame shows no real URL; use "health-provider site" in grey.

## Editing rhythm

- Change visual state every 4–7 seconds; a new reveal (fact, number, consequence, question) at least every 20–30 seconds.
- Hold the medical notice and each status stamp at least 3 seconds.
- Deep dive: slower cuts, longer holds on the silent-bell beat (2:20–2:30) — let the silence land.
- Signals: snappier, one module per signal; a 0.5 s navy breath between signals.
- Privacy Radar: one row per ~10 seconds, synced to narration.

## Accessibility and captioning

- Burned-in or uploaded captions for 100% of narration; verify spelling of Perry Johnson, Orrick, Beth Israel Deaconess, ABHA, Kolkata.
- Minimum on-screen text size 42 px at 1080p; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (white/amber on navy pass).
- Never encode risk by colour alone — every red/amber/teal marker also carries the word High / Medium / Low.
- No flashing above 3 per second; the red flash in Transition 2 is a single soft pulse.
- Provide an audio description-friendly narration: key on-screen numbers are also spoken.
