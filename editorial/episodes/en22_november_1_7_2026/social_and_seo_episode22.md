## LinkedIn post

Your health data is only as safe as the least visible company that handles it.

This week, the attorneys general of Connecticut, Michigan, New Jersey and New York announced a settlement with five Perry Johnson companies — transcription, coding and billing vendors to health-care organizations — over a 2023 breach that reached more than 13.3 million people.

According to the attorneys general:
• no multi-factor authentication for remote access
• files containing diagnoses, test results and Social Security numbers were unencrypted
• monitoring software recorded the intrusion but wasn't configured to alert anyone

None of these are exotic failures. They're basics — at a company most patients never knew existed. For health systems, it's a reminder that vendor oversight is patient safety. For patients, it's permission to ask: which outside companies handle my records, and how are they protected?
