## Cover A — "Watch meets cuff"

Design a final square 1920×1920 podcast episode cover for **LifeeCode Hindi**, Episode 20. Bold, premium editorial style matching the approved LifeeCode Hindi Spotify Art System v2.

- **Background:** deep textured midnight navy / black-blue, subtle grain, no gradients that look like a gadget ad.
- **Top:** cyan label "EPISODE 20" at top-left with thin cyan divider lines across the top.
- **Left hook:** short, large editorial hook "WATCH = BP?" — "WATCH =" in white, "BP?" in muted red. Bold condensed sans-serif.
- **Title:** below the hook, the full Devanagari-plus-Roman title in amber/gold, two or three lines, perfectly accurate: "क्या आपकी smartwatch आपका high BP पकड़ सकती है?"
- **Right-side illustration (cyan line-art):** a minimal smartwatch seen from the side with a thin pulse-wave (PPG) trace flowing out of its sensor, and the trace ending in a faint outline of a classic arm BP cuff with a small dial. A small gold question-mark node where the pulse trace meets the cuff, signalling "screening, not measurement".
- **Bottom lockup (fixed):** "LifeeCode Hindi" in white, then "Health Tech, Hindi Mein." in cyan.
- **Avoid:** faces, hands, hospital imagery, generic dashboards, numbers or BP readings on the watch face, hearts, clutter, YouTube-thumbnail styling, any medical claim.
- Must remain legible at small mobile size.
