# LifeeCode Weekly Research Prompt Template
## Copy-paste this into Manus each Monday

---

Find the 5 most important and impactful health technology news updates from the past week (last 7 days).

FOCUS AREAS:
- Wearable health devices (smartwatches, fitness trackers, CGMs, sleep trackers)
- Medical AI and diagnostics
- FDA approvals and regulatory updates
- Privacy and data security in health tech
- Longevity and biological age tracking
- Home health monitoring and biomarker testing
- Mental health technology
- Fitness and performance optimization tech

PRIORITIZE in this order:
1. Updates with privacy, security, or data protection angles
2. Evidence-based research and clinical studies
3. Accuracy and validation of health tech claims
4. Breakthrough innovations with real-world impact

FOR EACH UPDATE, PROVIDE:
1. Clear, descriptive title
2. 2-3 sentence summary (what happened, who's involved, key details)
3. Source link (credible news outlet or research publication)
4. "Why It Matters" explanation (1-2 sentences on impact for personal wellness)
5. The Futurist Lens: 1 sentence on what this signals for the next 5-10 years

ALSO PROVIDE:
- A short note stating whether there is one genuinely worthwhile **Global Signal** from outside the main domestic story set. If yes, give the title, 2-3 sentence summary, source, and why it matters strategically. If not, explicitly say that no strong global signal should be added this week.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
## Update 1: [Title]
**Summary:** [2-3 sentences]
**Source:** [URL]
**Why It Matters:** [1-2 sentences]
**The Futurist Lens:** [1 sentence]

[Repeat for all 5 updates]

## Global Signal Check
**Add a Global Signal this week?** [Yes/No]
**Title:** [If yes]
**Summary:** [2-3 sentences if yes]
**Source:** [URL if yes]
**Why It Matters Strategically:** [1-2 sentences if yes]

TONE: Professional, evidence-based, privacy-conscious, accessible to general audience.
