# LifeeCode Intro/Outro Script Options

## 60-Second Intro Options

### Option 1 — Personal Hook (Recommended for Episode 1 and milestone episodes)
*Use when: Introducing yourself to new audiences, milestone episodes, topic pivots*

"I've spent [X] years in cybersecurity — protecting systems, tracking threats, understanding how data moves. And when I started wearing a health tracker, I realised something uncomfortable: the same vulnerabilities I protect organisations from, I was now wearing on my wrist.

That's why I created LifeeCode. Every week, I cut through the health tech noise to bring you the five updates that actually matter — with a privacy-first lens that most health tech channels simply don't have.

This week, we're looking at [TOPIC]. [One sentence teaser of the most alarming or exciting finding.]

I'm Deveendra Murmu. Let's get into it."

**Word count:** ~110 words | **Pace:** Measured, confident | **Tone:** Personal, authoritative

---

### Option 2 — Question Hook (Use for high-stakes privacy or risk stories)
*Use when: The week's story has a strong alarming angle*

"Quick question: Do you know who has access to your health data right now?

Not just your doctor. Not just your insurance company. Your wearable manufacturer. Their third-party partners. Data brokers. And now, potentially, anyone willing to pay for it.

This week on LifeeCode, we're unpacking [TOPIC] — and what it means for every person wearing a smartwatch or fitness tracker.

I'm Deveendra Murmu. Here's what you need to know."

**Word count:** ~80 words | **Pace:** Punchy, urgent | **Tone:** Alert, direct

---

### Option 3 — Stat Hook (Use for research and study-led episodes)
*Use when: A compelling statistic anchors the week's main story*

"[Compelling statistic from the week's research — e.g., '78% of health apps share your data with third parties. That's not a guess. That's from a peer-reviewed study published this week.']

Welcome to LifeeCode — your weekly health tech brief, evidence-based and privacy-first.

This week we're covering [TOPIC], plus four other updates you need to know about.

I'm Deveendra Murmu. Let's decode what's happening."

**Word count:** ~70 words | **Pace:** Crisp, data-driven | **Tone:** Analytical, trustworthy

---

### Option 4 — Evergreen Template (Use from Episode 3 onwards as your weekly standard)
*Use when: Routine weekly episodes — change only the bracketed sections*

"Welcome back to LifeeCode — your weekly health tech brief, evidence-based and privacy-first.

I'm Deveendra Murmu, and every week I cut through the noise to bring you the five health technology updates that actually matter.

This week: [ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY OF THE DEEP DIVE TOPIC]. Plus [brief mention of 1-2 other stories].

Let's get into it."

**Word count:** ~60 words | **Pace:** Brisk, familiar | **Tone:** Consistent, reliable

---

## 20-Second Outro (Standard — record once, reuse every week)

"That's your LifeeCode brief for this week. If this was useful, subscribe so you never miss an update — and share it with one person who owns a wearable. I'm Deveendra Murmu. See you next week."

**Word count:** ~40 words | **Pace:** Warm, closing | **Tone:** Grateful, inviting

---

## Recording Tips

Record three takes without stopping to review between them. Your first take will feel stiff, your second will be better, and your third will be the one you use. Speak slightly slower than feels natural — what sounds slow to you sounds clear to listeners. Smile while recording — it comes through in your voice even in audio-only formats.

For audio-only YouTube: Record on your phone using the Voice Memos app (iPhone) or a free app like Easy Voice Recorder (Android). A quiet room with soft furnishings (bedroom, closet) reduces echo significantly. You do not need a professional microphone to start.
