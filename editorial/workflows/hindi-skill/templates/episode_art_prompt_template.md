# LifeeCode Hindi Episode Art Prompt Template

Use this template when generating square Spotify or podcast-platform episode art.

## Prompt Template

Design a final square podcast episode cover for **LifeeCode Hindi**.

Keep the established brand identity: a **premium, clean, trustworthy Indian health-tech podcast** with a **dark deep-indigo or near-midnight background**, a **polished editorial feel**, and **high legibility at small mobile sizes**.

Typography rules:
- **LifeeCode** should remain in one consistent brand font.
- **Hindi** should be differentiated tastefully, such as italic styling, while staying elegant.
- The bottom subtitle line should read **Health Tech, Hindi Mein** unless the user requests another approved variation.

Topic rules:
- Add a **single central symbolic visual** connected to the episode topic.
- Keep the topic image **minimal, calm, and premium**, not bright or flashy.
- Avoid clutter, meme-style composition, generic gadget advertising, or noisy thumbnails.
- Avoid tiny red heart accents unless the user explicitly requests them.

Listener rules:
- The art should feel aligned with an **Indian listener use case**, but express this symbolically rather than through a crowded literal scene.
- The art must feel **evidence-based, calm, credible, and privacy-aware**.

Compose the cover so it looks like a **polished podcast episode cover**, not a YouTube thumbnail.

## Fill-In Fields

| Field | Replace with |
|---|---|
| **{{TOPIC_VISUAL}}** | The central symbolic image for the episode |
| **{{TOPIC_CUE}}** | The practical listener issue, such as sleep score, stress alert, or AI symptom checker |
| **{{REFERENCE_IMAGE}}** | The currently approved show art to preserve brand continuity |
