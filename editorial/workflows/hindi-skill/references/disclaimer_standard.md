# Disclaimer Standard for LifeeCode Hindi

Use a **layered disclaimer system**.

## Default Rule

| Placement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Show description** | Full disclaimer |
| **Every episode description** | Short disclaimer |
| **Spoken disclaimer** | Only when the topic could be mistaken for diagnosis, treatment, medication advice, or symptom interpretation |

## Full Show Description Disclaimer

> **Disclaimer:** LifeeCode Hindi is an educational podcast about health technology, wearables, privacy, and digital health. The content is intended for general information and awareness only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it should not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. If you have symptoms, ongoing health concerns, or questions about diagnosis or treatment, please seek appropriate medical care.

## Short Episode Description Disclaimer

> **Note:** This episode is for educational purposes only and is not medical advice. If you have symptoms or health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Optional Spoken Line

> **Ek chhota sa note: yeh episode education ke liye hai, personal medical advice ke liye nahi. Agar aapko symptoms ya ongoing health concerns hain, toh qualified healthcare professional se baat karna important hai.**

## Tone Rule

The disclaimer should sound **calm, responsible, and human**. Never make it sound panicked, overly legalistic, or corporate.
